## What it says
- Lexical retrieval is already strong for questions that share words with their source: eight of nine types reach 0.78 to 1.00 hit rate at rank 10.
- The `semantic` type (a quarter of all questions) is where it fails: 0.456 at rank 10, because those questions paraphrase their source. This is the measured case for dense retrieval, and the next run adds it and fuses the two.
- This is a baseline, not our result: the gate is 0.99, and nothing here is tuned to any question.

## Limits
- Retrieval only; no model answered anything and no answer score is claimed.
- Documents are indexed by their first 40,000 characters; 600M tokens in total, so a few very long documents are truncated.
